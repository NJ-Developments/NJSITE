FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers to the questions we hear most.

What does NJ Developments do?

We build professional websites, set up AI voice agents that answer your calls 24/7, automate Google review requests after every job, and handle your SEO so customers find you on Google. Everything a local service business needs to get more customers without spending time on marketing.

How fast can you build my website?

Most websites go from concept to launch in 1-2 weeks. We move fast because you need to start getting leads, not wait around for months.

I already get work from referrals. Why do I need a website?

Referrals are great, but they have a ceiling. A website means people searching for your service in your area find you too. That's a second pipeline of leads you're currently missing. And when someone gets a referral, the first thing they do is Google you. If you don't show up, you lose the job before you know about it.

What's an AI voice agent?

It's an AI that answers your phone when you can't. If you're with a customer, driving, or it's after hours, the voice agent picks up, gets the caller's name, what they need, and their contact info, then texts it all to you. You call them back when you're free instead of losing them to a competitor who picks up.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Yes. We handle hosting, updates, and support on a monthly basis. You focus on running your business, we keep everything running online.
